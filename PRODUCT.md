Keep existing rules, DNS behavior, proxy keys and device exceptions on migration.

connection, and how to undo a change. Everyday actions must be usable on a phone.
Detailed diagnostics remain available without dominating the primary screen.

Mode: Operate. Visual direction: calm, precise home control panel with optional
technical detail, dark and light themes, local assets, Russian-first copy, native
LuCI compatibility. Do not include invented live metrics or hard-coded private data.

The user explicitly authorized a complete first version, role-based orchestrators
and subagents, installing relevant skills/plugins, and test-first development.
User-visible releases remain subject to completed checks and an explicit migration path.
